BEFORE YOU SWITCH ON

A few things
worth knowing.

No mystery about what happens next.

What do I need to get started?+

A YouTube channel or recurring long-form content, plus permission to repurpose the source videos. Bring your channel links and a sense of your audience; those give the agent a useful starting point.

How much will I need to do?+

You choose the sources, set the brand direction, and review the work. The agent handles the analysis, clip preparation, and content planning. The current workflow requires your approval before scheduling is enabled.

Do I have to keep uploading videos?+

Autopilot is designed to watch your connected source channels for new videos. You can also provide a YouTube video link or upload a source file. Use only videos you own or have permission to repurpose.

Will anything publish without my say-so?+

No. The current workflow is approval-first. You review the clips, captions, and calendar before content is scheduled. Automating the preparation does not grant permission to publish.

Can you guarantee channel growth?+

No one can guarantee how a platform distributes your content. Shoort Clips helps you build a consistent production and learning cadence. Views, subscribers, and leads depend on your channel, audience, offer, and content.

What happens after the free month?+

The pilot covers 30 days. If you’d like to continue, the regular Shoort Clips plan is $19.96 per month. Review the paid plan before subscribing. No credit card is required to apply for the pilot.
