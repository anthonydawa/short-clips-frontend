GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you hand over the channel.

Is this just an AI clip generator?+

No. Clipping is one step. Shoort Clips begins with a channel audit, creates clips against a content thesis, builds a test schedule, and uses performance signals to direct the next batch.

Will anything publish without my approval?+

No. The default workflow is approval-first. You review the clips, captions, CTA, and calendar before scheduling is enabled.

What do I need for the free pilot?+

A YouTube channel or business with recurring long-form video and permission to repurpose that content. The pilot form helps us confirm fit.

Can you promise a specific number of views or leads?+

No honest team can guarantee platform distribution. We guarantee the agreed production, testing, and reporting cadence. Your results will depend on the channel, offer, audience, and content.

Where is video processing handled?+

The frontend is designed to connect to your private processing API on Google Cloud. Uploads, jobs, progress events, clip files, analytics, and scheduling are separated behind documented endpoints.
