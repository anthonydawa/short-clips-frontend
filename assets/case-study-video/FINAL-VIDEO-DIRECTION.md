## Final format

- Runtime: 70 seconds
- Canvas: 16:9, 1920x1080 master
- Tone: polished platform launch, calm and evidence-led
- Structure: 10-second opening, 50-second client proof, 10-second close
- Brand: Shoort Clips red `#FF2D2D`, warm paper `#FBFAF7`, ink `#111111`, white
- Type: Manrope ExtraBold for headings, DM Sans Medium/Semibold for captions

Do not describe the outcome as guaranteed. During the analytics portion, keep a small footer visible: **Client analytics shown. Results vary by channel, audience, and content.**

## How to insert the screen recording

1. Generate the five frame-background clips for 0:10-1:00.
2. Put each generated background on the main timeline with its original Flow narration.
3. Place the screen recording above the background layer.
4. Scale and crop the recording to fill the blank dark display. Keep browser controls only when they help establish that this is real YouTube Studio data.
5. Round the recording corners to match the frame and add a very light soft shadow. Do not add perspective tilt.
6. For metric shots, crop closer than the full browser view. The numbers must remain readable in the landing-page embed.
7. Use a four-frame white flash or a fast red-line wipe only when moving from the channel page to analytics. Use simple eight-frame dissolves between analytics shots.
8. Keep burned-in captions to two lines maximum. Place them in a 70%-opaque ink-black pill near the bottom safe area and highlight no more than one phrase in red.

## Music and mix

- One continuous minimal electronic pulse at roughly 100-105 BPM.
- No cinematic trailer booms, corporate ukulele, or aggressive percussion.
- Add one soft interface click as the blank frame opens at 0:10 and one quiet resolving tone at 1:08.
- Keep the voice clearly forward; duck the music by about 8-10 dB beneath narration.
- Leave 0.4 seconds of visual stillness at the end so the CTA can be read.

## Claims and edit safeguards

- Use the exact visible data selected for the film: 42,070 views; 1.5K watch hours; +124 subscribers in the 365-day view; 338 current subscribers; +35 in the last 28 days.
- Do not say the channel had exactly 200 subscribers at takeover unless a dated screenshot proves it. The visible current and gained-subscriber data imply a roughly 200-level starting point, but the film should not present an inferred number as a verified timestamp.
- Do not say Shoort Clips guarantees views or subscribers.
- The rough recording shows a blocked-video notification on the dashboard. Crop it out whenever the dashboard is used; it distracts from the proof and is unrelated to the case study.
- Remove browser tabs and the green browser chrome on close metric shots when possible; retain enough YouTube Studio interface to preserve credibility.
