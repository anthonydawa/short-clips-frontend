Five supplied PNGs, six sequential 10-second clips: animated opening, three completely still proof clips, and a two-part animated closing. Reuse the closing image for clips 5 and 6. No live screen recording is needed.

## Important: preserving the proof

The proof PNGs contain real screenshots from the recording in the existing Shoort Clips frame. They have not been regenerated for this revision. Their entire canvas must remain still, including the frame, logo, charts, cursor, and thumbnails.

A prompt cannot guarantee that Flow will preserve screenshot pixels or small text. These prompts request a still image with voiceover, but the output must be checked. If Flow redraws or moves anything, use its narration and replace the whole picture with the matching original PNG, held for 10 seconds in your final editor. No zoom, pan, motion effect, or AI enhancement. This keeps the visual evidence intact while still using Flow for narration. Video compression may change pixel values, but the displayed evidence must not be regenerated or altered.

## Google Flow setup

- Use Video > Omni Flash > Ingredients, where available, and select 16:9 and 10 seconds.
- Upload the matching PNG and attach the same selected voice reference to every clip. Choose a voice that actually appears in your account; there is no requirement to find an Andrew preset.
- Recommended voice: a calm, warm male narrator, medium-low register, neutral English accent, clear diction, roughly 145–155 words per minute. Think a confident technology product presentation, without trailer drama or exaggerated sales delivery. Pronounce Shoort Clips as “Short Clips.”
- If you save a custom voice, name it Shoort Narrator and select it for every generation. Merely typing a name does not create or attach a voice.
- Keep “Return silent videos” off. Request narration only; no music or sound effects in individual clips.
- Use Ingredients for the reusable voice reference. Google currently documents voice references as Ingredients-only; do not combine the named-voice workflow with Frames mode.
- Generate each clip separately using its complete prompt below. Listen for the full sentence and check that it ends naturally before the clip ends. Do not speed up or truncate the evidence narration to force a take to fit.

## Finish the edit

1. Arrange the six clips in the order above, each lasting 10 seconds. The proof begins at 00:10 and ends at 00:40. The closing starts at 00:40; the direct CTA starts at 00:50.
2. Use simple cuts. No morphing, AI transitions, or motion effects between the proof images. Join clips 5 and 6 with a plain cut at similar resting compositions, or a very short dissolve if necessary.
3. Compare every proof clip against the original PNG. If any text, number, face, graph, cursor, or frame changes, retain Flow's audio and cover the entire clip with the original PNG for the full 10 seconds. Keeping this original-image overlay for ALL three proof clips is the safest final assembly.
4. Add a small static disclosure in the outer margin throughout 00:10–00:40: “Client analytics shown. Results vary.” Do not cover the screenshot. Add this in the editor, not through generative video.
5. Add simple, static closing typography in the empty left side. From 00:40: “Your agentic short-form growth team.” From 00:50 through the final frame: “Keep creating. We’ll handle the rhythm.” / “Get started at shoortclips.com”. Use Manrope for headings and DM Sans for support; warm white text and Shoort Clips red #FF2D2D accents. Ensure the exact URL spelling is shoortclips.com.
6. Optional: one quiet music bed across the full film, mixed well below the narration. Omit music entirely if you want the simplest assembly. Keep the final website address visible through the end.
7. Export a 16:9, 1920x1080 master. Publish the finished MP4 in the landing page's existing Client Proof video slot after reviewing the narration and visual evidence. This package does not modify or publish the website.
