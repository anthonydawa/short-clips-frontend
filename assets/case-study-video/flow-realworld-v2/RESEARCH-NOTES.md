# Creative reference notes — real-world revision

## What changed

The earlier device-and-light-path concept is replaced with a creator-led, interface-led commercial: an everyday problem, a visible editing action, a recognizable calendar, genuine client evidence, and the creator back recording.

## Sources inspected

1. [YouTube Create official product page](https://www.youtube.com/creators/youtube-create/): the presentation links approachable editing tools with the practical result of making and publishing videos.
2. [YouTube's step-by-step Create introduction](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/how-to-use-youtube-create/): describes the creator's editing burden and walks through recording, editing, captions and publishing. Its campaign still and editing illustration were inspected visually. They use actual-looking creator/video imagery, timeline controls and concise feature framing.
3. [YouTube Shorts creation-tool announcement](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/new-creation-tools-youtube-shorts-2025/): explains actions such as trimming, rearranging and previewing clips.

## Our creative interpretation

Borrow the communication pattern, not a shot-for-shot ad: recognizable work, one benefit per beat, sparse text, and a clear on-screen action. Natural creator B-roll is our addition to make the pain and payoff relatable. This is not a claim that every YouTube ad uses this style.

Use Shoort Clips' cream/black/red identity. Do not import YouTube Create's purple campaign styling, copy its artwork, imply endorsement, or depict Shoort Clips automation as a built-in YouTube feature.

The downloaded official reference images are research-only and are not included in the deliverable kit or used as public ad footage. New scene images were generated with the built-in image tool. The only documentary client visual is 04-real-client-analytics.jpg.
