# Real-world v2 — original image-generation prompts

Built-in image-generation tool; not CLI. Scene 1 was generated from text. Scenes 2, 3 and 5 used scene 1 as identity/content reference. Scene 4 is not generated; it is an unchanged copy of a real recording frame.

## 01-creator-problem

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: one 16:9 landscape starting image for a realistic creator-software commercial, approximately 1920x1080.
Primary request: a believable candid live-action shot, with a large recognizable simplified YouTube channel interface on a real desktop monitor. This must look like a frame photographed for a creator productivity advertisement, not an AI concept render.
Scene: a small everyday home recording studio in afternoon daylight. A female creator around 30 with medium tan skin, shoulder-length naturally wavy dark brown hair, a faded charcoal cotton t-shirt, sits at a pale wood desk. Seen in left profile and slightly over her shoulder; she occupies only the left quarter, looking at the screen with a mildly tired, thoughtful expression. Natural skin pores, flyaway hair, fabric texture, imperfect lived-in surroundings, no glamorous retouching. A small real camera and notebook sit on desk; modest setting.
Composition: monitor dominates center and right, nearly front-facing with believable slight perspective, completely readable broad interface structure. The monitor displays a clean light-mode YouTube channel page: small familiar YouTube wordmark at top left, search field, round profile avatar of this same creator, muted photographic channel banner, tab labels only "Videos" and "Shorts". Six genuine-looking landscape video thumbnails of this creator recording talking-head educational videos at her desk, in an orderly 3 by 2 grid, NO title text beneath thumbnails and NO subscriber/view counts. Selected Videos tab. Minimal gray separators, clean white background, actual practical website layout. Keep the interface sparse; avoid invented analytics or red declining arrows. Do not include a Shoort Clips button in YouTube's interface. This is a fictional demo channel, not a client's account.
A clean cream wall strip across the upper 15% allows a headline to be added later. Keep the creator and monitor in the lower 80%. No headline rendered in this image. Natural neutral colors, restrained small red accents from YouTube plus a red notebook. Soft window lighting, moderate depth of field so monitor and creator are both legible, actual DSLR still frame, subtle natural grain. Everything is physically on the desk.
Avoid: floating panels, holograms, glossy cubes, neon paths, robot mascots, 3D rendering, sci-fi objects, exaggerated unhappy acting, stock-photo grin, generic blue SaaS UI, giant play-button sculptures, unreadable paragraphs, watermarks, new words beyond the specified UI labels. One complete shot, not a storyboard or collage.
```
