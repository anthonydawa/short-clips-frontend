## Recommended setup

- Length: 10 seconds per screenshot
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Voice: `@Voice: Shoort Narrator`
- Visual input: upload the matching PNG as the visual ingredient/reference
- Use the same model, voice, aspect ratio, and seed/variation settings for all five shots
- Generate narration only; add one continuous music track during the final edit

The YouTube interface, thumbnails, channel identity, dates, charts, words, and numbers are evidence. They must remain unchanged. The excitement should come from camera motion, Shoort Clips border lighting, focus outlines, and sound design—not from changing the screenshot.

## If Google Flow changes the evidence

Reject any generation that alters a number, label, chart, thumbnail, or date. For maximum fidelity, generate the motion without voice using the PNG as both the first and last frame, then place the approved Shoort Narrator audio underneath in the final editor. A subtle push-in on the untouched original PNG is preferable to an impressive animation with inaccurate analytics.
