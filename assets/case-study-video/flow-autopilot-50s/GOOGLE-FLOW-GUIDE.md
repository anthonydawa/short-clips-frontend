# Shoort Clips — 50-second Google Flow production guide

> Superseded creative direction: use [the real-world creator film v2](../flow-realworld-v2/GOOGLE-FLOW-GUIDE.md). It replaces glossy AI-device imagery with natural creator B-roll and simplified practical interfaces.

Final direction: five sequential 10-second shots. Four animated product scenes plus an animated presentation of one authentic client screenshot. No live screen recording is needed.

## Start here

Use the five PNGs in this folder, numbered 01–05. Each has a matching PROMPT-01.txt through PROMPT-05.txt: attach that PNG and your saved voice, then paste the entire matching prompt. These prompts are repeated in full below. Do not paste all five prompts into one generation.

The references and prompts are ready; the actual videos and narration have not been generated or auditioned. Expect to review and possibly regenerate a shot.

## Flow settings and narrator

1. Create one project: Shoort Clips — Autopilot.
2. Choose Video → Omni Flash → Ingredients if available in your account. Set 16:9 and 10 seconds. Keep "Return silent videos" OFF.
3. Upload only the relevant numbered image for each shot as the primary visual ingredient. Reuse the same voice reference every time.
4. In Add → Voices, preview your available voices. Choose a warm, clear medium-low male voice. Do not look for "Andrew" specifically; a preset by that name is not available in your account.
5. If Create New Voice is available, choose your preferred existing base voice, name the new voice "ShoortNarrator", and use this Voice Performance direction:

> Warm, assured male technology-product narrator. Medium-low register, neutral international English with clear American-style diction. Calm authority, a slight smile, precise consonants, natural breaths, and restrained excitement. Conversational product-launch delivery around 150–165 words per minute, without a movie-trailer growl or influencer-style hype. Pronounce "Shoort Clips" as "Short Clips." Keep a consistent pace and timbre.

Use this sample dialogue when previewing: "Keep creating. Leave the clips, the schedule, and the learning to your agent."

Select/attach that saved voice for all five shots. Typing its name in ordinary text is not a substitute for attaching the voice reference. Where supported, type @Voice and choose it from Flow's selector.

Voice references require Ingredients, not Frames. These images guide composition, not an exact first frame. If those controls are unavailable, use your available image/video mode with the same voice direction and review for drift. [Google: video, image and voice references](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353334?hl=en)

Ten-second Omni Flash generations and the silent-video setting are documented by Google. [Google: project settings and formats](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16935308?hl=en)

## Assembly

1. Generate in order 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, using the corresponding full prompts below or the separate TXT files. Review one scene before spending credits on variants of all five.
2. Add the five approved clips in numerical order to a timeline. Target cuts at 10, 20, 30 and 40 seconds, total 50 seconds. Use clean cuts, with the red light direction providing continuity. No forced morph from a product card into the real analytics interface.
3. If Flow's Scenebuilder is available, arrange and trim there. Download for final overlays and, if required, proof correction in your editor. [Google: edit and build scenes](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16935718?hl=en)
4. Keep each voice line complete. If it clips or rushes, regenerate that shot; do not speed it up mechanically just to fit.
5. Add the suggested headline overlays using Manrope bold, supporting copy in DM Sans. Place them in available negative space with generous margins. Keep the UI-inspired images illustrative, not labelled as actual app screens.
6. On shot 3, add the small line "You approve. The agent runs the plan." This matches the current website's approval-first promise while emphasizing automated clipping, planning and scheduling.
7. On shot 5, place the headline and shoortclips.com in the empty left half. Hold the CTA for at least the final three seconds. The final narration says "short clips dot com"; the visible spelling must be "shoortclips.com" with two o's.
8. Optional: add one continuous, understated electronic music bed under the whole film after assembly. Do not ask five separate generations to invent five music tracks. Keep narration dominant.
9. Export a landscape MP4 at 1920×1080 if your generated resolution supports it, using a consistent frame rate. Do not stretch the references; preserve their aspect ratio.
10. Insert the finished film in the landing page's existing product-tour section (#proof). The new product-tour copy is live on shoortclips.com; the placeholder correctly says the film is coming soon until a playable film exists.

## Publication check

- Same voice, logo shape, palette and agent device throughout.
- All narration words are present and intelligible.
- No claims of guaranteed views, guaranteed subscribers, or posting without the approval the site promises.
- Real analytics unchanged; period correctly described as Last 365 days.
- Repurposing story is a reported client example, not a controlled attribution study.
- CTA spelling correct; captions readable on mobile.
- Final runtime approximately 50 seconds; evidence shown at 30–40 seconds.
- Client has cleared public marketing use of the identifiable analytics before publication.
