PRODUCT AND AUDIENCE
Shoort Clips helps YouTube creators repurpose existing long-form videos into Shorts. Its agentic AI identifies useful moments, cuts vertical clips, plans posting frequency and timing around the audience, schedules approved content, and uses performance feedback to improve future decisions. The core benefit is less repetitive editing and scheduling, and more time to create. Sell autopilot with creator control—not guaranteed growth or instant virality. Approval comes before scheduling. Shoort Clips is independent software, not an official YouTube product.
